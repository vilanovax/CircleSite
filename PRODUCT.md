# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: someone with a real need (buy, sell, find a specialist, ask for advice) who would rather start with people they already know than with strangers on a public board.

Secondary: someone offering a thing, a service, or a lead who wants it to reach a trusted circle first.

## Product Purpose

Circle (سیرکل) is a classifieds-and-connection app. People publish needs, requests, offers, and ads inside a ring of friends, colleagues, and acquaintances, then reach the right person through real relationships. Success for this marketing site is a first-time visitor understanding that idea in seconds and entering the app.

## Positioning

Circle is not another public classifieds wall and not another social network. It routes a need along real, trusted connections. Some ads can stay anonymous until the publisher chooses to reveal their identity to a specific person or conversation.

Brand line in use: «از حلقه آشناها شروع کن.»
Other confirmed lines: «آنچه نیاز داری، شاید در حلقه آدم‌های نزدیک تو باشد.»

## Operating Context

The product lives in the app at https://app.mycircle.ir. This repository is the public marketing landing that explains the idea and sends people into that app. Visitors arrive on a phone first.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed in the product:

- Publish ads and requests
- See ads from one’s circle
- See the path of connection between people
- Chat directly about an ad
- Hide name and identity on some ads
- Publish anonymously
- Reveal identity only to chosen people
- Manage privacy and who can see an ad

Undecided: legal entity name, support email, app store listings, real product screenshots.

## Brand Commitments

Name: سیرکل / Circle. Domain in use: mycircle.ir.

Voice: short, intimate, direct Persian. No marketplace hype, no social-network cheerleading.

Visual constraints from the brief: minimal, modern, quiet; people, relation, and trust over clutter; simple lines or circles for connection; one consistent icon language; mobile first. Do not present Circle as Divar or as a new social network.

Primary CTA label is fixed: «ورود به سیرکل» → https://app.mycircle.ir

## Evidence on Hand

No official logo file, no real app screenshots, no testimonials, no member counts, no press. Hero UI and people in the constellation are authored demonstrations and must not be presented as live product metrics or real users.

## Product Principles

1. Start from people you already know, not from a public crowd.
2. Trust is the product difference; closeness is the mechanism.
3. Identity is a choice the publisher keeps.
4. Say the idea once, simply; then send people into the app.
5. Never invent social proof, prices, or capabilities that were not confirmed.
